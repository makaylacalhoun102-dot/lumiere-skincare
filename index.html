05 / BEHIND THE DESIGN

A little more than beautiful.

Hi, I'm Makayla — the designer behind LUMIÈRE.

I created LUMIÈRE as an original, fictional skincare brand to explore how thoughtful visuals and useful interactions can work together. I wanted the experience to feel soft, expressive, and polished without sacrificing clarity.

From the illustrated packaging and responsive layout to the personalized ritual quiz, product stories, and demo shopping bag, every detail is part of a hands-on web design portfolio project.
